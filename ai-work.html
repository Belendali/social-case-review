How the workflow runs

  1. Decide — problem, direction, scope. Mine.
  2. Design — Figma, 1:1 with the real product; icons and spacing exported, never hand-drawn by AI.
  3. Build — AI turns the board into a running prototype in hours, not sprints.
  4. Judge — on a real phone, with friends; dozens of rounds of “not yet” until it’s right.
  5. Keep — my taste written down as reusable skills, so the next product starts further ahead.
