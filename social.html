Streaks, stickers, group chats — the pieces I can see serve two people already talking. I didn’t find much that holds the space between shares, but I may well be missing it.

What I’d want to know first

Five questions I’d want to ask first.

How much of a share ends up outside the app?Sizes the leak. If most of it lands in iMessage, the work isn’t a new surface — it’s keeping the reply here.
How many DM threads get past three turns?Tells me whether DMs are a conversation or a delivery pipe. Those are two different products.
Is Friends-tab time taken from FYP, or new?If it cannibalises, friend content has to clear a higher bar. If it’s additive, it has earned its slot.
Of people who posted once, how many posted again?First post to second post is the honest read on whether posting to friends has a reason to exist here.
How many mutuals does a normal user have?Graph density decides what’s even possible. Presence needs a handful of mutuals, not thousands.

I don’t want to walk in with answers before I know the numbers — you have them and I don’t. So these are the five things I’d want to ask about first.

How much of a share ends up outside the app — that sizes the leak. How many DM threads get past three turns — that tells me if DMs are a conversation or a delivery pipe. Whether Friends-tab time is taken from For You or new. How many people who posted once posted again. And how many mutuals a normal user actually has.

Each answer changes what I’d design — which is the same thing I did at Answer.AI: find where it leaks first, then decide what to fix.
