The read on TikTok

A share every day, and no one is served after it.

TikTok already creates the urge to send something to one person. The chain breaks halfway — and the half that breaks is the social half.

Sees itThe strongest recommendation engine in the world.
Thinks of someoneThe motive already exists.
Sends itA large share of it leaves for iMessage or WeChat.
The conversationHappens in another app.
Comes back to replyThe hardest reason to open, and TikTok doesn't own it.

Streaks, stickers, group chats — every piece TikTok has built serves two people who are already talking.

说：TikTok 每天制造上亿次"想发给某个人"的冲动，但这条链路在第三步出了 app。它已经做了 streak、贴纸、群聊、语音通话——但这些服务的都是已经在聊天的两个人，没有一个服务刚刚分享完的那个人。
