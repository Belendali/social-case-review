My read

Three things I believe about this.

01 · What it is for

The friend graph is a retention layer, not a content layer

TikTok already wins attention with strangers. Friends don't need to supply content — they need to supply a reason to come back.

02 · Where the energy is

The share is the social act

The product already creates the urge to send something to one person. That impulse is the strongest social asset it has.

03 · What's missing

Nothing holds the space between shares

Between two sends there is no lightweight way to be present with someone. Not another feed — a sense that they're there.

占位：三条判断，每条一句话讲透。

Where it breaks

A share every day, and no one is served after it.

The chain starts inside TikTok and finishes somewhere else — and the half that leaves is the social half.

Sees itThe strongest recommendation engine in the world.
Thinks of someoneThe motive already exists.
Sends itA large share of it leaves for iMessage or WeChat.
The conversationHappens in another app.
Comes back to replyThe hardest reason to open, and TikTok doesn't own it.
